## Spending money (frugal)

Before anything is bought, subscribed to, upgraded, renewed or booked, and before you choose a paid
API, SaaS, cloud resource, LLM model or domain, run a frugal check sized to the amount: free or
already-owned options, the right plan size and billing period, startup, student, open-source and
nonprofit programs, verified promo codes and cashback, and cheaper alternatives that do the same job.
Report the best options in a few lines with sources, then let the user choose. Never invent prices or
codes, never suggest anything against a vendor's terms, and never pay without the user's explicit yes.
Full method: the `frugal` skill (https://github.com/fainir/frugal).
